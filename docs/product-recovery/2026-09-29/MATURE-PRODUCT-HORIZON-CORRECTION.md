# Mature product horizon correction — DINOForge

Date: 2026-09-30.
Authority: USER INTENT CORRECTION / RECOVERED USER INTENT.
This document corrects product framing; it does not change the frozen implementation candidate.

## Accepted intent

DINOForge is fundamentally a **general DINO modding platform/framework**, not merely a hot-reload/content-registry project and not primarily a warfare product.

Recovered user-intent anchors:
- 2026-03-10: scope explicitly shifted to a general DINO mod platform/framework first; warfare was the flagship domain rather than the product boundary.
- 2026-09-30: goal restated as opening the modding libraries/surfaces so essentially everything useful can be changed, including total conversions such as Star Wars and modern-conversion mods.

## Mature falsification journey

A mature DINOForge should make an ambitious total conversion possible without requiring the mod author to maintain a fork of the underlying game merely because a necessary gameplay/content surface is closed.

The mature contract therefore eventually needs to account for all materially required surfaces discovered through archaeology, including but not limited to content definitions, behavior/rules, factions, economy, AI/doctrine, maps/world/scenarios, UI, assets/rendering/audio, animation/effects, progression, persistence compatibility, and runtime lifecycle.

This list is a discovery agenda, not a mechanically accepted requirement list.

## Current recovery slice

The current GenerationStore/reload program is foundational because universal moddability is not credible if accepted content generations cannot converge under change/removal/restart.

It is **one slice** of the mature product, not the product definition.

The ten RegistryManager domains and runtime-consumer denominator remain valid subordinate work.

## Evidence discipline

Do not promote assistant-originated domain ideas into accepted product obligations without user/repo/history support. Total-conversion journeys should be used to discover missing surfaces rather than to manufacture a feature quota.
